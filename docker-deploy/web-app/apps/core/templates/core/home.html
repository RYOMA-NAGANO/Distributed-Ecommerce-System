Ship, track, and lab integration

MiniUPS uses Postgres and the World simulator. After you sign up, log in with your email (no password complexity rules). Use My shipments to track status. Shipments are created by Amazon over the AUIP API; the UPS daemon drives trucks in the World simulator.

What you can do

  • Sign up / Log in: email + password (demo accounts).
  • AUIP: Amazon calls UPS (POST /shipments, pickup, ready, loaded, etc.); UPS callbacks notify Amazon at each step.
  • My shipments: track status, filter, and search.
